Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026  ·  App: Invoicer

1. Overview

Invoicer is a freelancer invoice and receipt tool. Your data stays on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal information.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Business information you enter (name, address, logo)
  • Client names and contact details
  • Invoice and receipt data (amounts, line items, dates)

3. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat is used to process and verify in-app purchases. RevenueCat may collect your purchase transaction ID and subscription status. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers

5. Permissions

Photo library: Only accessed when you choose to upload your business logo. The image is stored locally on your device only.

6. Data Deletion

All app data can be deleted by uninstalling the app. No data is stored on external servers by us.
